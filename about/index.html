About KNOWPATH

KNOWPATH / PROJECT-EVOLVE was founded on a simple principle: human capability development must be grounded in demonstrable evidence, transparent mathematics, and total user agency.

The Problem with Traditional EdTech

Modern online education relies almost universally on passive video completion rates, vanity streaks, and self-assessed confidence. Meanwhile, generative AI chatbots answer questions enthusiastically but often hallucinate capabilities that learners do not actually possess.

When candidates enter technical interviews or production environments, missing foundational concepts lead to immediate failure and frustration.

Our Non-Negotiable Charter

  • ✓
    Deterministic Scoring: LLMs paraphrase concepts and tutor learners, but never calculate mastery scores. Scores derive from closed-form Bayesian Beta distribution updates.
  • ✓
    Strict Evidence Hierarchy: Self-reports are never upgraded to assessment proof without genuine test execution.
  • ✓
    No Outcome Guarantees: We never promise jobs, salaries, or grade outcomes. We measure demonstrated knowledge with rigorous confidence intervals.
  • ✓
    Right of Dispute: If an assessment question or score is contested, learners can file an auditable dispute directly in the app.

Data Sovereignty & Age Protection

In compliance with global data protection principles and COPPA/GDPR age rules, KNOWPATH enforces a strict age gate requiring users to be 18 or older during Phase 1.

All learners possess full export rights (JSON machine-readable archive) and hard account deletion rights with complete cryptographic wipe of personal records.
