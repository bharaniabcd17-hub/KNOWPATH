How KNOWPATH Works

From onboarding to verifiable capability proof: a continuous, mathematically rigorous loop.

1

Age Verification & Explicit Consent

You complete the date-of-birth age gate (18+ only in Phase 1) and inspect our explicit consent screen detailing exactly what evidence is captured and how your model evolves.

2

Adaptive Diagnostic Assessment

Take a 15-item adaptive assessment. Rather than random questions, our information-gain selector serves items at your knowledge frontier to calibrate your baseline across core Python concepts.

3

Cognitive Twin Instantiation

Your responses immediately update your Cognitive Twin. Each concept receives a Beta distribution mastery score, credible interval, and transparent evidence trail.

4

Topological Gap Detection & Roadmap

Choose your target goal ("Python for Placements" or "Python for Data Work"). Our gap engine traverses the DAG to output an ordered roadmap with foundation bonuses for concepts that unblock downstream dependencies.

5

Daily Missions & Spaced Revision

Receive a focused 15-45 min daily mission tailored to your available hours, reinforced by an SM-2 spaced retention schedule so acquired concepts don't decay.
